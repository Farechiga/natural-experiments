## Editorial notes

The intro follows the first book's interest in dependence and practical knowledge into the work of rebuilding a community: learning to provide food, sharing limited resources, and examining accusations before acting on them. The harvest and working hands are interpretive images. Both communities face real pressures; the script leaves open who caused the damage and how the conflict is resolved.
