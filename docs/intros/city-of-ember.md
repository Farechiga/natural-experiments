## Editorial notes

- The generator represents inherited capability without enough surviving understanding to sustain it. Ember's workers still perform repairs; the script does not portray them as wholly unskilled.
- Electric light and understanding separate: the lights can remain on while the knowledge needed to maintain a civilization fades. This is our interpretive image.
- Goethe supplies a reversal: an apprentice cannot stop his enchanted helper, while Ember cannot keep its machine working. The returning master in the poem makes the Builders' absence more troubling.
- The AI question concerns a possible loss of practised judgement and independent capability. It does not claim that AI caused Ember's predicament or inevitably weakens its users.
- The damaged message suggests incomplete inherited knowledge. Lina and Doon's observation and interpretation give the warning a hopeful direction.
- The AI research below reports associations and self-reported effort, not proof of long-term skill loss or societal decline. It informs the framing, rather than entering the spoken script as a factual prediction.
